## ⚙️ Technical Details Made Simple

You don't need to be a programmer, but here's some helpful background:

**What is OpenClaw?** It's an AI agent built with Node.js, meaning it runs efficiently on most computers. It works with local language models (LLMs) - so your AI can think and respond without needing cloud services if you prefer.

**What are these "topics"?** You might see words like "MCP," "agentic-ai," or "autonomous-agents." Don't worry - these just describe what makes OpenClaw special. MCP stands for Model Context Protocol, which is simply a way for OpenClaw to share information with other programs. Agentic AI means the assistant can take actions on its own when you ask it to.

**Do I need special hardware?** awesome-openclaw works well on typical Windows computers. If you plan to use local AI models, a newer processor and at least 8GB of RAM will give you the best experience. But you can also connect OpenClaw to cloud-based AI services if you prefer.
